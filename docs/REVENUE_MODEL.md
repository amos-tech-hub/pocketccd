# PocketCCD Revenue Screen

Snapshot date: 2026-11-12

## Assumptions

- Initial business model: free trial camera plus lifetime unlock, with the option to test a low monthly price later.
- Observed competitor lifetime prices cluster around roughly $14.99–$19.99; PocketCCD should initially test $9.99–$14.99.
- For screening, use a monthly-equivalent net revenue per payer of $3.40 after Apple's small-business commission and blended product behavior.
- Organic acquisition is assumed. Paid acquisition is not justified by this model.
- Twelve-month cumulative figures assume linear growth from zero to month-12 MAU; actual launches will be lumpier.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Month-12 MAU | Paid conversion | Net monthly-equivalent per payer | Month-12 net | Approx. 12-month cumulative |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 5,000 | 1% | $3.40 | $170 | $1,105 |
| Base | 25,000 | 2% | $3.40 | $1,700 | $11,050 |
| Upside | 100,000 | 3% | $4.25 | $12,750 | $82,875 |

The base scenario narrowly passes the product screen: month-12 monthly net exceeds $1,500 and approximate cumulative net exceeds $10,000. The result is not strong enough to support broad paid acquisition or a large content operation.

## Decision

**Conditional GO.** PocketCCD is economically interesting only if its distinct output and sharing formats generate organic acquisition. A generic CCD filter clone is a NO-GO because it would require distribution strength the project does not currently possess.

Re-evaluate after the first external test using actual activation, D7/D30 retention, share rate, and unlock conversion.
